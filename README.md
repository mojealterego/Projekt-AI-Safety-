# Projekt AI Safety

Research infrastructure for automated auditing of cooperative AI systems, based on the project proposal *Wniosek Grantowy AI Safety Singapore*.

## Research tracks

1. Normative environments: PDDL, Markov games, bounded rationality, and model organisms.
2. Goal inference: SIPS and CLIPS, with calibrated uncertainty.
3. Introspection: LoRA adapters, DPO refinement, and negative controls.
4. Robustness: adversarial distribution shifts, ADT, and OOD evaluations.
5. Governance: reproducible audit reports, limitations, and policy translation.

**Status:** repository initialization. No trained model, validated detector, or grant award is claimed.
